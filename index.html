Why it matters

AI builders are great at features. They don't ask about auth.

They optimize for "it works." Nobody's job in that loop is to ask who's allowed to call this route, or where that key ends up.

!

Keys ship in the bundle

Service-role keys and live API secrets get hardcoded because that's the fastest path to "it works" — and it does, right up until someone reads your client JS.

?

Routes trust whoever calls them

Checkout, admin, and account routes get generated without an auth check, because the prompt never mentioned one.

$

Prices come from the client

Amounts, roles, and quantities get read straight from the request body and trusted, because recomputing them server-side is a whole extra step.
